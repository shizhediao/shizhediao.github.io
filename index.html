I am now a third-year undergraduate at Beijing Normal University, majoring in Computer Science. With great academic performance (GPA = 3.94/4.0), I'm passionate about artificial intelligence, natural language processing and how it can be improved and inspired by the human brain, and I have decided to pursue further studies at doctorate level after graduation in July 2019.

Undergraduate as I am, I have published a SCI indexed paper and a journal paper about data mining for finance text. Worked as the captain, I organized a team to participate in the World Robot Olympic (WRO) and won the second place in India.

As an intern at The Language Computing and Web Mining Group, advised by Xiaojun Wan, I focus on proposing a novel framework to generate Chinese poems automatically for any essay, such as news, and I have submitted one paper for 2018 Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing as the first author.

I'm now a summer intern at WING Group, supervised by Prof. Min-Yen Kan and Wenqaing Lei. I'm working on nearal dialogue generation with semi-supervision.
