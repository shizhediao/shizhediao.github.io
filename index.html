I am now an undergraduage at Beijing Normal University, majoring in Computer Science. As an intern at Peking University, I'm now working with Xiaojun Wan. I am full of curiosity about my major and have published a SCI paper. As a captain, I led my teammates to participate in the World Robot Olympic (WRO) and won the second place in India. My main research interests lie within deep learning for natural language processing. More recently, I have been working on natural language generation. I'm concentrating on building a classical Chinese quatrain generator based on the RNN encoder-decoder framework.
