Shizhe Diao

I am now a final-year Ph.D. candidate at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, advised by Professor Tong Zhang. Currently, I am a visiting scholar in BLENDER LAB@UIUC, under the supervision of Prof. Heng Ji. I received my Master of Philosophy degree at HKUST in 2021.

Before joining HKUST, I received my Bachelor of Science degree at Beijing Normal University in 2019. I was a research intern at ByteDance AI Lab with Dr. Hang Li and Dr. Xinsong Zhang, and Sinovation Ventures AI Institute. During my undergraduate study, I interned at WING Group @ NUS and PKU, where I was fortunate to work with Prof. Min-Yen Kan and Prof. Xiaojun Wan. My research focuses on pre-training, efficient-tuning, and adaptation of large language models.
