Here is my Curriculum Vitae

I am now a fourth-year undergraduate at Beijing Normal University, majoring in Computer Science. With great academic performance (GPA = 3.94/4.0), I'm passionate about artificial intelligence, natural language processing and I have decided to pursue further studies at doctorate level after graduation in July 2019.

Undergraduate as I am, I have published a SCI indexed paper and a journal paper about data mining for finance text. Worked as the captain, I organized a team to participate in the World Robot Olympiad (WRO) and won the second place in India.

As an intern at The Language Computing and Web Mining Group of Peking University, advised by Xiaojun Wan, I focus on designing a novel framework to generate Chinese poems automatically for any essay, such as news. I am preparing one paper for IJCAI 2019 as the first author.

I was a research intern at Web Information Retrieval Natural Language Processing Group (WING) of National University of Singapore, supervised by Prof. Min-Yen Kan and Wenqiang Lei. I was working on neural dialogue generation with semi-supervision.
