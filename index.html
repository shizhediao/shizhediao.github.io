More About My Recent Research

I'm now focusing on the Natural Language Generation and I have spent half a year to build a classical Chinese quatrain generator based on the RNN encoder-decoder framework.

Two 4-layer LSTM networks are used as encoder and decoder respectively. The encoder takes as input four keywords provided by a poem planner, and the decoder generates a quatrain character by character.
